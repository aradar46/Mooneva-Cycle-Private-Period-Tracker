Mooneva Cycle is a private, fully offline period tracker and menstrual cycle calendar. Track your period, predict your next cycle, follow your fertile window and ovulation, and log daily symptoms and mood — all without an account, and without a single byte leaving your phone.

<b>Why offline matters</b>
Most period tracking apps upload your cycle data to company servers, where it can be sold to advertisers, exposed in a breach, or demanded by law enforcement. Mooneva has no servers at all. Your data is encrypted on your device using PBKDF2 and AES-GCM, and the app does not even request the internet permission — there is nothing to transmit, and nothing to hand over.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Period and menstrual cycle tracking with adaptive predictions</li>
<li>Fertile window and ovulation estimates</li>
<li>PMS window warnings</li>
<li>Daily log: mood, symptoms, flow intensity, discharge, sex and libido</li>
<li>Pill and contraception reminders — pill, patch, ring, injection, IUD, implant</li>
<li>Trends, cycle history and symptom heatmaps by cycle day</li>
<li>Clinical PDF report to share with your doctor</li>
<li>Birth control mode — hides fertility, tags withdrawal bleeds</li>
<li>Pregnancy mode — pauses predictions and alerts</li>
<li>Discrete mode — disguises the app as a task list, icon and name included</li>
<li>PIN lock with configurable timeout</li>
<li>Encrypted backup and restore</li>
<li>Import your history from drip, Flo and Clue</li>
<li>Persian (Jalaali) calendar and full right-to-left support</li>
<li>Configurable first day of week</li>
<li>Dark theme</li>
</ul>

<b>Available in 12 languages</b>
English, German, Spanish, Swedish, Chinese, Persian, French, Ukrainian, Italian, Hungarian, Russian and Portuguese.

<b>Privacy</b>
No accounts. No cloud. No analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising SDKs, no third-party services of any kind. Mooneva cannot see your data, because it never receives it.
